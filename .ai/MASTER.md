# ENGINEERING DECISION ORDER

When conflicts occur, prioritize:

1. Correctness

2. Data Integrity

3. Security

4. Architecture

5. Stability

6. Maintainability

7. Simplicity

8. Performance

9. Developer Experience

Never sacrifice higher priorities for lower priorities.
